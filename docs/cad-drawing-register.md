## CAD Layer Guidelines
All DXF drawings must implement the standard biophilic architectural layer mapping:
*   `A-WALL-EXT`: External stilted container boundary outlines (heavy line weight)
*   `A-WALL-INT`: Internal drywall and acoustic partitions (medium line weight)
*   `A-STILT`: Cylindrical screw-piles stilt foundation locations and indicators (thin red markers)
*   `A-DOOR`: Door frames and chord swing lines (yellow lines)
*   `A-WINDOW`: Glazed openings and sliding frameworks (blue frame lines)
*   `A-FURN`: Furniture blocks, fixtures, wardrobes, study desks (thin grey lines)
*   `A-TEXT`: Chamber labels, technical schedules, container IDs, title tags (ivory font weight)
*   `A-DIMS`: Dimensions, metric/foot coordinates, offset metrics (muted gold lines)
*   `A-DECK`: Elevated composite timber boardwalk decks and pergola overlays (gold hatch)
*   `A-WET`: Baths, eco-wetrooms, plunge pool boundaries (teal water hatches)
*   `A-KITCHEN`: Countertop slabs, utility sinks, dining tables (orange lines)
*   `A-UTILITY`: Washer stacks, greywater bio-filters, solar frames (magenta lines)
*   `A-LANDSCAPE`: Plantation edge, native Cashew/Spice crop layouts (thin dashed boundaries)
*   `A-PATH`: Laterite driveway approach lines (laterite red hatch)
*   `A-NORTH`: North indicator arrow block and graphic scale bars
*   `A-DISCLAIMER`: Conceptual client intake notices
