# Visual Production Specification: 2BHK Compact Forest Estate (Phase 2D-A)

This production checklist defines the exact technical modeling, rendering, styling, and export specifications required to produce and replace the first cycle of **2BHK Compact Forest Estate** visual assets.

---

## 1. AutoCAD 2D Presentation Floor Plan
AutoCAD drafters must output a clean, high-contrast, biophilic vector drawing that highlights the luxury ground-level modular spatial planning.

### Technical Export Specifications
*   **Target Output File:** `/images/floorplans/2bhk-floor-plan.svg`
*   **Palette Standards (Premium Presentation Colors):**
    *   **Background:** Deep charcoal (`#1A1A1A`) or rich forest green (`#10291F`).
    *   **Vector Line Work:** Pure gold (`#B08A3E`) or gold light (`#D4B66A`).
    *   **Text & Labels:** Soft warm ivory (`#FBF8F1`).
*   **Required Architectural Elements:**
    *   **Residence Footprint:** Highlight the ground-level container structural framing (~6% of the 1-acre plot).
    *   **Composite Timber Decks & Verandahs:** Clear hatching patterns displaying the biophilic wrap-around walkways (~9%).
    *   **Open-to-Sky Courtyard:** Central courtyard containing native plants and laterite stone drainage borders.
    *   **Managed Agroforestry Bounds:** The cashew/spice planting matrix border wrapping around the residence block (~63%).
    *   **Foyer access path:** Laterite stone driveway and vehicle parking ports (~12%).
    *   **Utility Buffers:** Solar battery panels, water sumps, and bio-septic placement (~10%).

---

## 2. SketchUp 3D Massing & Scene Calibration
The 3D modeling team must calibrate the **2BHK Compact Forest Estate** massing model to verify the stilted screw-pile orientation, sun-angles, and volumetric structures.

### Modeling Requirements
*   **Target Model Naming:** `Reserva-Verde-2BHK-Compact-Forest-Estate.skp`
*   **Structural Frame:** Model exact high-cube containers (40ft and 20ft modules) stilted on cylindrical screw-piles raised 1m to 1.5m above the natural forest topography.
*   **Roof Pitch:** Insulated double-roof assemblies set with a standard pitch of 5° to 7° to optimize rain runoff and solar PV cell orientation.
*   **Scene Camera Setup (1 to 8 Matching Renders):**
    1.  `Scene-1: Exterior Arrival` — Front 3/4 perspective showing the entry bridge corridor.
    2.  `Scene-2: Deck View` — Courtyard perspective showing floating decks and pool link.
    3.  `Scene-3: Living Room` — Eye-level wide shot inside the glazed living container looking out.
    4.  `Scene-4: Bedroom View` — View from master suite highlighting dual corner glazing.
    5.  `Scene-5: Bathroom View` — Stilted wetroom shot framing the private window garden.
    6.  `Scene-6: Kitchen/Dining` — Professional show kitchen focusing on the central island.
    7.  `Scene-7: Night View` — Warm LED atmospheric downlighting scene setup.
    8.  `Scene-8: Aerial Estate` — AXONOMETRIC 45-degree angle showing the stilted unit nested inside its cashew forestry context.

## 3. Lumion / D5 Render / Twinmotion High-End Visualizations
The rendering team is tasked with producing photorealistic visualizations. The visual theme must convey high-comfort eco-sensitive HNI luxury.

### Rendering Constraints & Guidelines
*   **Resolution Specs:** Minimum 1920x1080 (Full HD), 3840x2160 (4K UHD) highly preferred.
*   **Required Filename Contracts:**
    *   `exterior-arrival.jpg` -> `/images/renders/2bhk/exterior-arrival.jpg`
    *   `deck-view.jpg` -> `/images/renders/2bhk/deck-view.jpg`
    *   `living-room.jpg` -> `/images/renders/2bhk/living-room.jpg`
    *   `bedroom-view.jpg` -> `/images/renders/2bhk/bedroom-view.jpg`
    *   `bathroom-view.jpg` -> `/images/renders/2bhk/bathroom-view.jpg`
    *   `kitchen-dining.jpg` -> `/images/renders/2bhk/kitchen-dining.jpg`
    *   `night-view.jpg` -> `/images/renders/2bhk/night-view.jpg`
    *   `aerial-estate.jpg` -> `/images/renders/2bhk/aerial-estate.jpg`
*   **Atmospheric & Landscaping Standards:**
    *   **Mood:** South Goa forest mood. Muted early-morning mist or soft golden sunset hour light.
    *   **Foliage:** Densely packed local flora. Cashew groves, piper nigrum vines, valley ferns, and wild ridge bamboo.
    *   **Strict Exclusions:** NO European pines or generic cypress models. NO urban background skylines. NO hyper-reflective skyscraper-style chrome panels.
