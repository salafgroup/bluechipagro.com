# Visual Asset Specification & Brief: 2BHK Final Render Exports (Phase 2D-B)

This specification guide acts as the single point of direction for the rendering and geospatial visual production teams to finalize the active visual replacements of the **2BHK Compact Forest Estate** collection.

---

## 1. Lumion / D5 Render / Twinmotion Visual Brief
These instructions outline the precise framing, material choices, lighting values, and visual constraints required for the remaining 4 renders.

### Technical Rendering Guidelines
*   **Resolution Output:** Minimum 1920x1080 pixels; 3840x2160 (4K UHD) highly preferred.
*   **Format:** High-density `.jpg` files.
*   **Aesthetic Quality Constraint:**
    *   **Mood:** Deep South Goa Netravali biophilic wilderness context.
    *   **Lighting:** Warm, organic, indirect light setups. Emphasize early morning sun breaks, mist filters, and amber LED night sweeps.
    *   **Strictly Avoid:** Generic urban city villa architecture, hyper-bright neon lighting grids, excessive glossy chrome paneling, fake luxury branding objects, or non-native landscaping assets (e.g. American maples, generic pine forests).
