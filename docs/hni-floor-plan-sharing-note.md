# Reserva Verde Goa — HNI Floor Plan Sharing Note

## Dear Valued Buyer,

Thank you for choosing **Reserva Verde Goa** as your premier biophilic forest retreat. To support your design exploration and provide absolute transparency, we have prepared a supplementary set of traditional black-and-white plotted architectural floor plans.

These sheets have been drafted to mimic professional AutoCAD-style working blueprints, providing a clear technical view of the 2BHK Compact, 3BHK Premium, and 4BHK Signature Forest Estates. 

As we prepare this review package, please keep the following technical guidelines and covenants in mind:

### 1. Conceptual Design Intent
These architectural floor plans are **conceptual support designs** outlining the space layouts, stilted modular footprints, composite timber boardwalk deck orientations, and agroforestry boundaries. They serve to demonstrate biophilic flow, furniture allocations, and room sizing relationships.

### 2. Plotted Dimensions
All dimensions, room sizes (expressed in both meters and feet/inches), screw-pile piling coordinates, and built footprints are **indicative and subject to final survey**. 
*   **Perimeter Bounds:** Overall width and length values represent conceptual chassis frames.
*   **Chamber Layouts:** Room metrics (e.g. Master Bedroom, Living lounge, chef kitchen) outline inside-to-inside clearances.

### 3. Statutory & Site Engineering Dependencies
Final construction footprints and technical layouts remain fully dependent on:
*   Licensed topographic land surveys and soil bearing bore tests.
*   Licensed architect stamp reviews and structural engineer designs.
*   Goa TCP (Town & Country Planning), RERA registration, environmental buffer clearances, and local panchayat statutory approvals.

### 4. How to Use this Package
We invite you to review these drawings to:
*   Examine the **room-to-room spatial flow** and structural snugs.
*   Understand the **size logic** and L-shaped stilted pavilion geometries.
*   Evaluate your preferred model configuration relative to your 1-acre agroforestry estate allotment.

*All dimensions are indicative and subject to final architectural survey. Not for civil construction.*
