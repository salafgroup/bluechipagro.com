### Column Dictionary & Coding Reference

1.  **Name:** Lead name.
2.  **Location:** Primary residence city (e.g., Mumbai, Bengaluru, London, Dubai).
3.  **Buyer Type:** Individual HNI / Corporate Family Office / Institutional Investor / NRI.
4.  **Interested Model:** 2BHK Compact / 3BHK Premium / 4BHK Signature / Customisable Founder Estate.
5.  **Budget Range:** ₹1.95 - 2.35 Cr / ₹2.35 - 2.65 Cr / ₹2.65 - 2.99 Cr / ₹2.99 Cr+.
6.  **Purpose:** Personal Retreat / Managed Agriculture Investment / Weekend Getaway / Family Legacy / Founder Estate.
7.  **Visuals Feedback:** Log responses to 4K renders, stilt structures, interior layouts, and topography site plans.
8.  **Pricing Feedback:** Responses to standard base packages, structural upgrades, and dynamic milestone schedules.
9.  **Questions / Objections:** Note legal queries, zoning questions, ecological clearance checks, or architectural modifications.
10. **Follow-up Date:** YYYY-MM-DD for the next action.
11. **Priority:** **HOT** (Ready to reserve) / **WARM** (Follow-up scheduled) / **COLD** (No immediate interest).
12. **Assigned Staff:** Lead relationship manager.
13. **Notes:** Specific qualitative observations (e.g., *Vaastu compliance requested on custom positioning*).
