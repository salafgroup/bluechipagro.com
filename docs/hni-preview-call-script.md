# HNI Private Preview — Client Onboarding Call Script

This call script is designed for private client relations directors, portfolio advisors, and HNI sales coordinators presenting *Reserva Verde Goa*. It emphasizes architectural intelligence, ecological integrity, and absolute compliance.

---

## 1. Opening & Premium Positioning

**Advisor:** 
> *"Good morning/afternoon [Client Name], this is [My Name] calling from Bluechip Global Agro's private client desk. I hope you are doing well.*
> 
> *I am calling to share a private, invitation-only preview of our flagship ecological forest project in South Goa: **Reserva Verde**.* 
> 
> *We have just launched a secure, live digital portal featuring detailed 3D site plans, 4K render galleries, and topographic survey mappings for a select group of investors and early founders. Knowing your appreciation for private estate sanctuaries and eco-sensitive architecture, I wanted to present this layout to you personally before our wider public launch."*

---

## 2. Introducing the Reserva Verde Vision

**Advisor:** 
> *"Reserva Verde is a low-density, stilted eco-retreat nestled inside Neturlim Valley, South Goa. We are not developing a crowded residential enclave. Instead, we are offering an organic forest retreat centered on ecosystem preservation.*
> 
> *Each estate allocation is exactly **1-Acre of private land**, with approximately 63% of the plot dedicated to actively managed organic cashew and spice agroforestry. The homes themselves are designed as single-storey, stilted modular structures built on helical screw piles. This means our foundations float above the forest floor, preserving the natural hydrology, mature tree root zones, and the organic topography of the valley."*

---

## 3. Guiding the Prospect Through the Four Models

**Advisor:** 
> *"Our virtual catalog features four distinct design typologies tailored for Neturlim Valley. If you open the portal link I have sent, you can view them in the **Estate Models** section:*
> 
> 1.  **2BHK Compact Courtyard (~₹1.95 Cr):** *A U-shaped design wrapped around an open-to-sky biophilic courtyard, using insulated steel structures and high-performance low-E glass.*
> 2.  **3BHK Premium Pavilion (~₹2.35 Cr):** *An L-shaped ridge-aligned retreat featuring wrap-around composite timber observation decks and a central glass spine.*
> 3.  **4BHK Signature Retreat (~₹2.65 Cr):** *A flagship three-wing retreat featuring double-height lounge spaces, Corten steel framing, and a courtyard pool observatory.*
> 4.  **Customisable Founder Estate (~₹2.50 Cr onwards):** *A completely bespoke layout that grants you full creative freedom to align custom modular volumes alongside our managed cashew agroforestry.*
> 
> *In each brochure page, you can open the **Interactive 2D Site Plan Explorer** to examine the exact spatial layouts and ecological zoning allocations."*
