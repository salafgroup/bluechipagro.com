## 2. Technical Production Specifications

### A. 2D AutoCAD Floor Plan Layout
*   **Required Target File:** `/images/floorplans/customisable-floor-plan.svg` (and backup transparent PNG).
*   **Color Palette Contract:** High-contrast luxury presentation style. Deep black/forest green background (`#10291F`), grid lines and margins in gold (`#B08A3E`), annotations and room names in clean ivory (`#FBF8F1`).
*   **Drafting & Spatial Requirements:**
    *   Show a flexible, modular ground-level footprint that balances physical spatial layout with bio-centric constraints.
    *   Highlight a modular zone split showing **Social Living Pavilion**, **Founder's Master Wing**, **Guest Wings (3BHK / 4BHK expansion profiles)**, **Central Wellness Courtyard Garden**, **Wrapping Timber Deck & Plunge Pool**, and **Off-Grid Utility Hub**.
    *   Include clear, readable labels and a "customisable / indicative / subject to final bespoke design" disclaimer within the vector drawing stamp.
    *   Keep it presentation-grade for high-net-worth (HNI) buyers—avoid dense engineering dimension lines or plumbing cross-sections.

### B. SketchUp 3D Model Setup
*   **Required SKP File Name:** `Reserva-Verde-Customisable-Founder-Estate.skp`
*   **Scenes List:** Camera placements must align to these 8 matching perspectives to support consistent web-gallery swaps:
    1.  Founder arrival
    2.  Deck / wellness garden / pool view
    3.  Grand living lounge
    4.  Master suite
    5.  Signature bathroom / wetroom
    6.  Kitchen / dining / entertaining zone
    7.  Night view
    8.  Aerial custom estate layout
*   **Scale Standard:** 1:1 metric units. Stilted cylindrical pilings, preserving site topography and wild root systems.

### C. Lumion / D5 / Twinmotion Render Specifications
*   **Required Renders:** 8 individual high-fidelity renders mapped to `/images/renders/customisable/`.
*   **Resolution Specification:** Minimum 1920x1080 pixels; 3840x2160 (4K UHD) highly preferred.
*   **Format Specification:** Clean, web-compressed `.jpg` extension.
*   **Lighting Profiles:** Soft diffused monsoonal morning breaks or warm dusk sweeps.
*   **Aesthetic Exclusions:** Strictly **no city elements**, neon lighting, high-rises, or generic luxury clutter. Focus strictly on South Goa's wild bamboo, ferns, cashew trees, and spice crop integration.

## 3. Scene-by-Scene Creative Direction

The Customisable Founder Estate represents the ultimate tier of bespoke private retreats at Reserva Verde, emphasizing extreme privacy, flexible footprints, and curated natural living.

### 1. Founder Arrival
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/customisable/exterior-arrival.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** A grand but understated private entry leading to a bespoke pivot timber door link. Winding laterite pathways lead through a dense cashew canopy. Frame a stilted modular architecture with charcoal steel outlines floating over Netravali contour slopes.
*   **Key Elements:** cylindrical stilt pilings, wild bamboo, corten steel, private gate feeling. No city backgrounds or neon.

### 2. Deck & Wellness Garden View
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/customisable/deck-view.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** A massive composite timber deck wrapping around stilted pavilions. Show premium lounge seating, a stilted plunge pool with reflective evening twilight ripples, and a dedicated biophilic wellness garden pocket.
*   **Key Elements:** Amber pathway low-glows, Composite timber planks, Netravali ferns, calm resort-level HNI retreat mood.

### 3. Grand Living Lounge
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/customisable/living-room.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** Double-height grand social hall under structural cedarwood rafter beams. Double-glazed sliding panels frame espectacular canopy views, with cleft laterite accent panels and premium organic minimalist furniture.
*   **Key Elements:** Bamboo wood flooring, natural linen, cleft laterite stone, high ceilings.

### 4. Founder Master Suite
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/customisable/bedroom-view.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** A massive forest-facing master suite wing. Glazed sliding walls open directly onto a wrapping deck, framing sun sweeps over the valley ridge.
*   **Key Elements:** Warm indirect LED lights, low-profile bed, private writing snugly, refined fabrics.

### 5. Signature Master Ensuite
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/customisable/bathroom-view.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** High-end eco-wetroom featuring raw slate/laterite walls, floating double teakwood vanities, ribbon glass privacy planting, and a freestanding copper soaking tub.
*   **Key Elements:** Brushed copper rain shower fixtures, natural light well, privacy spice shrubs outside.

### 6. Kitchen & Entertaining Zone
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/customisable/kitchen-dining.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** Spacious chef's island dinette ideal for premium entertaining. Features timber cabinetry with gold trims, a seamless quartz counter island, and large dining table.
*   **Key Elements:** DGU windows streaming morning monsoonal light, Managed spice beds outside.

### 7. Night Atmospheric Profile
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/customisable/night-view.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** A highly cinematic night render of the stilted modular pavilion in the dark Netravali forest. Warm low-glow 3000K amber spot lighting emphasizes stilt pillars and rafter contours.
*   **Key Elements:** Pathway marker spots, soft glow through glass corridors. Strictly no neon or artificial luxury clutter.

### 8. Aerial Custom Estate Layout
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/customisable/aerial-estate.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** 45-degree angle drone axonometric view showing flexible spatial layouts, 1-acre private agroforestry grids (Mango, cashew, pepper), stilt elevations, utility solar buffer, and access pathways.
*   **Key Elements:** Footprint configuration options, cashew plantation layout, valley topography boundaries.
