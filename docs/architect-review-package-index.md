# Reserva Verde Goa — Architectural Floor Plan Review Package Index

This document serves as the master index for the **Reserva Verde Goa** Traditional 2D Architectural Floor Plan Review Package. It provides an overview of the technical drawing sheets generated for the 2BHK, 3BHK, and 4BHK Forest Estates, details the exchange file paths, and outlines specialized review checklists for licensed architects, structural engineers, and TCP/local approval consultants.

---

## 1. Technical Drawing Package Overview

The Traditional 2D B&W Architectural Floor Plan Package represents a comprehensive pre-construction design cycle designed to supplement the dynamic green/gold HNI presentation drawings. All exchange files (DXF) are plotted in real-world meters utilizing a standard architectural drafting scale factor of `1m = 70px`, allowing seamless imports into AutoCAD, Revit, SketchUp, and Lumion. Standalone plotted SVGs feature high-contrast black-and-white layouts on white background sheets, complete with technical double borders, graphic scales, north arrow symbols, space schedules, and blue-grey dimension chains (`#1f4e79`) with centered metric/feet text and diagonal slash ticks.

## 3. Specialized Technical Review Checklists

### A. Licensed Architect Review Checklist
Reviewers in this discipline are requested to check the following parameters:
- [ ] **Spatial Flow & Circulation:** sanity check room accesses, foyer entries, deck thresholds, and relative privacy buffers.
- [ ] **Door/Window Schedules:** verify that door widths (hinged and sliding) and window glazed sashes comply with regional residential codes.
- [ ] **Agroforestry Setbacks:** verify the plantation borders, Cashew/Spice buffer margins, and stilted structure setbacks from forest borders.
- [ ] **Agroforestry Ratios:** verify that the built footprint does not exceed localized agricultural zoning buffer values.
- [ ] **Utility Zones:** verify structural clearances for laundry hubs, solar battery arrays, and off-grid water digesters.

### B. Licensed Structural Engineer Review Checklist
Reviewers in this discipline are requested to check the following parameters:
- [ ] **Screw-Pile Stilt Nodes:** audit the structural spacing of the galvanized stilt piling locations relative to container modular loads.
- [ ] **Stilt Foundation Crosshairs:** verify coordinates of stilt nodes relative to the structural axis coordinates.
- [ ] **Composite Timber Decks support:** check deck boundaries and structural cantilever points on perimeter stilts.
- [ ] **Active Plunge Pool Loads:** evaluate structural framing coordinates for the stilted plunge pool water loads (e.g. 80 sq.ft pool in 2BHK, 180 sq.ft pool in 3BHK).
- [ ] **Soil bearing assumptions:** cross-reference assume structural pile depth with upcoming topographical soil bore tests.

### C. TCP & Local Panchayat Approval Consultant Checklist
Reviewers in this discipline are requested to check the following parameters:
- [ ] **Statutory Setbacks:** review front, rear, and side setback margins relative to Goa TCP guidelines.
- [ ] **RERA compliance:** verify that all area metrics mapped in the space schedules comply with RERA carpet area definitions.
- [ ] **Environmental Buffers:** ensure modular pavilions are strictly situated outside local environmental conservation streams or forest reserve boundaries.
- [ ] **Eco-Sanitation:** verify greywater bio-filter locations relative to local public health rules.

## 4. Technical Disclaimer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **CONCEPTUAL PLANS ONLY — STRICTLY NOT FOR SITE CONSTRUCTION**
> All drawings, coordinate lists, screw-pile centers, spatial zoning buffers, room schedules, and dimensions presented in this package are conceptual biophilic design supports. They do not constitute stamped civil, structural, environmental, or panchayat execution blueprints. Final layouts and dimensions remain contractually subject to licensed site surveys, topographic checks, structural engineer soil reviews, and statutory TCP, RERA, and local panchayat approvals.
