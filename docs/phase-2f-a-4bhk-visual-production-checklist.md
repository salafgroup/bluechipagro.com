## 2. Technical Production Specifications

### A. 2D AutoCAD Floor Plan Layout
*   **Required Target File:** `/images/floorplans/4bhk-floor-plan.svg` (and transparent PNG backup).
*   **Color Palette Contract:** High-contrast luxury presentation style. Deep black/forest green background (`#10291F`), grid lines and margins in gold (`#B08A3E`), annotations and room names in clean ivory (`#FBF8F1`).
*   **Drafting Requirements:**
    *   Flagship three-wing single-storey stilted modular layout.
    *   Delineate the following zones clearly: **Grand Living Pavilion**, **Family Dining Room**, **Chef's Open Kitchen**, **Flagship Master Suite**, **Bedroom 2 (Guest Suite)**, **Bedroom 3**, **Bedroom 4**, **Ensuite Bathrooms**, **Wellness Garden Courtyard**, **Wrap-around Forest Deck**, **Stilted Plunge Pool**, **Utility & Solar Hub**, **Plantation Edge**, and the **Laterite Entrance Road**.
    *   Keep it presentation-grade for high-net-worth (HNI) buyers—avoid dense engineering dimension lines, plumbing details, or cross-sections that clutter vector layouts on-screen.

### B. SketchUp 3D Model Setup
*   **Required SKP File Name:** `Reserva-Verde-4BHK-Signature-Forest-Estate.skp`
*   **Scenes List:** Camera placements must align to these 8 matching perspectives to support consistent web-gallery swaps:
    1.  Exterior arrival foyer
    2.  Pool/deck/wellness garden view
    3.  Grand living room facing forest
    4.  Master bedroom suite
    5.  Signature bathroom wetroom
    6.  Family kitchen/dining
    7.  Night atmospheric view
    8.  Aerial masterplot view
*   **Scale Standard:** 1:1 metric units. Raised strictly on cylindrical stilt pilings to reflect low-impact, terrain-sensitive biophilic architecture.

### C. Lumion / D5 / Twinmotion Render Specifications
*   **Required Renders:** 8 individual high-fidelity renders mapped to `/images/renders/4bhk/`.
*   **Resolution Specification:** Minimum 1920x1080 pixels; 3840x2160 (4K UHD) highly preferred.
*   **Format Specification:** Clean, web-compressed `.jpg` extension.
*   **Lighting Profiles:** Diffused monsoonal light, sunset golden sweeps, or warm dawn breaks.
*   **Aesthetic Exclusions:** Strictly **no city elements**, neon lighting, high-rises, or foreign vegetation. Focus strictly on South Goa's wild bamboo, ferns, cashew trees, and black pepper crop integration.

## 3. Scene-by-Scene Creative Direction

The 4BHK Signature Forest Estate represents the ultimate flagship villa of the collection, balancing a massive footprint with low-impact biophilic design.

### 1. Exterior Arrival & Foyer Link
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/4bhk/exterior-arrival.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** A grand arrival view winding through laterite walkways and dense forest canopy. The modern stilted modular architecture floats over Netravali valley contours, leading to a private gate entrance and a solid teak pivot wood foyer link.
*   **Key Elements:** Elevated cylindrical stilt piles, corten steel, warm timber cladding, dense cashew trees. No city backgrounds.

### 2. Deck & Wellness Garden View
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/4bhk/deck-view.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** An expansive family stilted deck projecting into the forest edge. Features a stilted plunge pool, integrated wellness garden pocket, comfortable composite lounge chairs, and warm evening twilight reflections.
*   **Key Elements:** Ambient low-glow pathway spots, composite timber deck planks, privacy vegetation.

### 3. Grand Living Room
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/4bhk/living-room.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** A high-ceiling family living lounge under structural cedarwood rafter beams. Double-glazed insulated panels frame spectacular multi-aspect vistas of the South Goa canopy.
*   **Key Elements:** Bamboo wood flooring, warm organic linen fabrics, a feature panel of cleft laterite stone, premium furniture.

### 4. Flagship Master Bedroom Suite
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/4bhk/bedroom-view.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** A massive private stilted wing facing the sunrise ridge. Sliding glass apertures open directly onto the wrapping verandah, framing active bamboo leaves.
*   **Key Elements:** Refined biophilic fabrics, low-profile bed, writing study snug, warm indirect LED light washes.

### 5. Signature Master Ensuite Wetroom
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/4bhk/bathroom-view.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** A resort-level eco-wetroom featuring a raw laterite wall backdrop, double teakwood floating vanity deck, and freestanding copper tub looking directly into forest cashews.
*   **Key Elements:** Ceil-mounted rain shower corridor, natural privacy planting behind ribbon glass, brushed copper fixtures.

### 6. Family Open Kitchen & Dining
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/4bhk/kitchen-dining.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** Large family kitchen and dinette ideal for premium entertaining. Features a composite stone counter island, timber cabinetry with gold trim accents, large dining table under designer warm pendant lights.
*   **Key Elements:** DGU window slots streaming monsoonal morning light, spice garden context outside.

### 7. Night Atmospheric Profile
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/4bhk/night-view.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** A highly cinematic night render of the stilted pavilion amidst the dark Netravali jungle. Low-glow 3000K amber spots trace the stilts and deck outlines.
*   **Key Elements:** Ambient pathway markers, soft glowing glazed interiors. Strictly no neon or overbright searchlights.

### 8. Axonometric Aerial Plot View
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/4bhk/aerial-estate.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** A 45-degree angle drone orthographic concept detailing the grand stilted three-wing villa footprint, pool deck, wellness courtyard, access road, off-grid utility buffer zone, and managed agroforestry crops (Teak and Coffee) within the 1-acre plot bounds.
*   **Key Elements:** Comprehensive zoning footprint, stilt elevations, cashew plantation grids.
