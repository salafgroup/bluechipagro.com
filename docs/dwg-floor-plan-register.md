## 2. Technical Layer Specifications & Standards

The generated binary DWG assets carry standard, uncorrupted architectural drafting layer properties:
*   `A-WALL-EXT`: Heavy external modular container contours (color 7, plotted thick).
*   `A-WALL-INT`: Internal room drywall and acoustic boundaries (color 8, plotted medium).
*   `A-STILT`: Galvanized pile foundation coordinates (color 1, red circles with crosshairs).
*   `A-DOOR`: Door sashes and hinge arcs / double sliding pathways (color 9, light grey).
*   `A-WINDOW`: Glazed openings and sliding screen borders (color 4, cyan).
*   `A-FURN`: Furniture blocks including double beds, pillow sets, seating snugly, dining sets, closets (color 9, light grey).
*   `A-TEXT`: Space chamber annotations and area values (color 7, black/white font).
*   `A-DIMS`: Blue-grey dimension chains with centered metric/feet text and diagonal slash ticks (color 5, blue).
*   `A-DECK`: Elevated boardwalk timber decks (color 8, dark grey).
*   `A-WET`: Baths, wetrooms, and plunge pool water bounds (color 4, cyan).
