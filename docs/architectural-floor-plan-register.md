## Plotted Drawing Layout Guidelines

All traditional B&W architectural drawings must correspond to standard AutoCAD plotting aesthetics:
1.  **White Background Sheet** (`#ffffff`) for clean contrast and printing compatibility.
2.  **Double Sheet Border** with dashed inner margins to mimic traditional plotted drawing frames.
3.  **Standard Layers Mapping**:
    *   `A-WALL-EXT`: External container outer walls, stilted boundaries (Thick black outline, light grey solid fill).
    *   `A-WALL-INT`: Internal room partitions (Medium dark-grey, light grey fill).
    *   `A-STILT`: Cylindrical screw-piles stilt foundation locations (Red markers with crosshairs).
    *   `A-DOOR`: Door sashes and hinged swing arcs / double tracks (Muted grey swing dash).
    *   `A-WINDOW`: Glazed openings and sliding frameworks (Cyan / Blue double frames).
    *   `A-FURN`: Furniture blocks, closets, beds, seating snugly (Thin light grey outlines).
    *   `A-TEXT`: Chamber labels, container IDs, sheet notes (Bold black/grey font).
    *   `A-DIMS`: Dimensions, metric/feet equivalents, slash ticks (Navy Blue lines with centered text).
    *   `A-DECK`: Elevated composite timber boardwalk decks and verandahs (Thin grey hatch).
    *   `A-WET`: Baths, eco-wetrooms, plunge pool boundary (Cyan pool water hatch).
    *   `A-KITCHEN`: Countertops, sinks, utility counters (Thin grey).
    *   `A-UTILITY`: Laundry hubs, solar modules, off-grid water frames (Thin grey).
    *   `A-NORTH`: North indicator arrow block and graphic scale bars.
    *   `A-DISCLAIMER`: Conceptual technical review disclaimer.
