## 2. Technical Production Specifications

### A. 2D AutoCAD Floor Plan Layout
*   **Required Target File:** `/images/floorplans/3bhk-floor-plan.svg` (and transparent PNG backup).
*   **Color Palette Contract:** High-contrast luxury presentation style. Deep black/forest green background (`#10291F`), grid lines and margins in gold (`#B08A3E`), annotations and room names in clean ivory (`#FBF8F1`).
*   **Drafting Requirements:**
    *   U-shaped/L-shaped single-storey stilted modular layout.
    *   Clearly delineate the following zones: **Living Room (Glass Spine)**, **Dining Area**, **Open Family Kitchen**, **Master Bedroom Suite**, **Bedroom 2 (Guest Suite)**, **Bedroom 3**, **Ensuite Bathrooms**, **Wrap-around Forest Deck**, **Utility & Solar Hub**, **Plantation Edge**, and the **Laterite Entrance Path**.
    *   Maintain highly clean presentation standards; avoid engineer-level technical annotation blocks, plumbing cross-sections, or dense dimension lines that clutter vector layouts on high-resolution screens.

### B. SketchUp 3D Model setup
*   **Required File Name:** `Reserva-Verde-3BHK-Premium-Forest-Estate.skp`
*   **Scene Constraints:** Camera placements must align to 8 matching perspectives to support consistent web-gallery swaps.
*   **Scale Standard:** 1:1 metric units. All container modules (high-cube 40-ft and 20-ft envelopes) must be raised strictly on cylindrical stilt pilings to reflect low-impact, terrain-sensitive biophilic architecture.

### C. Lumion / D5 Render / Twinmotion Constraints
*   **Required Renders:** 8 individual high-fidelity renders mapped to `/images/renders/3bhk/`.
*   **Resolution Specification:** Minimum 1920x1080 pixels; 3840x2160 (4K UHD) highly preferred.
*   **Format Specification:** Clean, web-compressed `.jpg` extension (zero spaces in filenames).
*   **Lighting Profiles:** Emphasize early morning dawn breaks, diffused monsoon mist, or soft sunset ambient sweeps.
*   **Aesthetic Exclusions:** Strictly **no city elements**, neon light bars, generic modern swimming pools, high-rise buildings, or foreign vegetation (e.g. European maples). Focus strictly on South Goa's Netravali wild bamboo, native ferns, mature cashew trees, and black pepper crop integration.

## 3. Scene-by-Scene Creative Direction

The 3BHK Premium Forest Estate represents an upgraded family-size biophilic luxury model. While maintaining a zero-footprint stilted approach, it features expanded deck areas and spacious lounge frames.

### 1. Exterior Arrival & Foyer Link
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/3bhk/exterior-arrival.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** A high-contrast dawn-light perspective showing the stilted L-shaped container modules suspended over local valley topography. The laterite entry path winds through mature cashew trees, leading to a massive pivot teak wood door and double-glazed vestibule foyer.
*   **Key Elements:** Corten steel support grids, stilt piles, dense forest canopy backdrop. No urban skylines.

### 2. Verandah & Plunge Pool Deck
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/3bhk/deck-view.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** A family-sized composite wood deck projecting into the valley, wrapping around the master suite wing. Features a low-impact stilted plunge pool or lounge deck under filtered sunset light.
*   **Key Elements:** Ambient under-deck lighting, soft deck furniture, native Netravali ferns.

### 3. Biophilic Glass Spine Lounge
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/3bhk/living-room.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** An eye-level wide perspective of the central spacious living room. Dual-aspect double glazing frames active forest views. Open-beam cedar wood rafters support a high ceiling.
*   **Key Elements:** Polished bamboo flooring, raw linen fabrics, warm light sweeps, organic forest silhouette.

### 4. Master Bed Chamber Suite
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/3bhk/bedroom-view.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** Premium forest-facing master suite with direct sliding-glass access to the verandah deck. Corner window panels frame spectacular valley vistas.
*   **Key Elements:** Low-profile bed with organic linen, warm indirect cove LED lights, laterite stone texture wall, zero urban details.

### 5. Ensuite Master Wetroom
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/3bhk/bathroom-view.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** High-end stilted bathroom wetroom with a slate wall backdrop and ceiling-mounted rain shower.
*   **Key Elements:** Teak wood floating vanity, brushed copper hardware, native privacy planting outside the ribbon window, resort-level organic diffuse lighting.

### 6. Family Island Kitchen & Dining
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/3bhk/kitchen-dining.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** Spacious, open family kitchen and dining layout. Active natural morning sun streams through double-glazed side openings.
*   **Key Elements:** Brushed granite island breakfast counter, premium timber cabinetry with gold accents, large dining table under warm designer pendant lights.

### 7. Night Atmospheric Profile
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/3bhk/night-view.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** An ambient nighttime rendering highlighting the architectural silhouette stilted amidst the pitch-black jungle.
*   **Key Elements:** Warm amber downlighting (3000K) tracing the pile stilts, subtle pathway markers, glowing glazed interiors. Strictly no neon or over-bright floodlights.

### 8. Axonometric Aerial Plot View
*   **Filename Slot:** `/images/renders/3bhk/aerial-estate.jpg`
*   **Creative Brief:** Axonometric 45-degree angle drone orthographic masterplot visual displaying the entire 3BHK footprint in its 1-acre private agroforestry land envelope (~43,560 sq.ft).
*   **Key Elements:** L-shape module footprint, stilted structures, larger pool deck, cashew agroforestry grids, access route, and off-grid utility buffer zone.
