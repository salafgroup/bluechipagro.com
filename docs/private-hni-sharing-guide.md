## 2. 5-Minute Virtual Walkthrough Protocol

Follow this sequence to present the project effectively in under 5 minutes:

### Minute 1: The Vision & Location Advantage
*   **Zoning & Context:** Open the Homepage. Point to the **Survey 109 Land Intelligence Atlas** and the **Leaflet map**.
*   **Key Narrative:** Emphasize the ultra-exclusive 1-Acre private buffer. We are not selling dense villas; we are offering an organic forest retreat centered on ecosystem preservation in South Goa's Neturlim valley canopy.

### Minute 2: Architectural Experience Typologies
*   **Model Selection:** Scroll to the **Estate Models** section.
*   **Key Narrative:** Guide the buyer from the **2BHK Courtyard Residence** (~₹1.95 Cr) up to the flagship **Customisable Founder Estate** (~₹2.50 Cr onwards). Explain that each single-storey layout is designed on stilted pile foundations to prevent tree-root damage.

### Minute 3: Interactive Floor Plans & Galleries
*   **Visual Mappings:** Click **Open Full Model Brochure Page** for the prospect's interested model (e.g., 4BHK or Customisable).
*   **Key Narrative:** Show the **Interactive 2D Site Plan Explorer**. Click through the *Residence*, *Deck*, and *Cashew Agroforestry* tabs. Open the **4K Render Gallery** and click on thumbnails to demonstrate bedroom and bathroom designs wrapped by double-glazed glass walls.

### Minute 4: Transparent Public Pricing
*   **Calculators:** Open the **Villa Pricing** page.
*   **Key Narrative:** Adjust the payment schedule sliders. Show how transparent the pricing is, showing exact base costs, modular infrastructure fees, and structured milestone installment plans.

### Minute 5: Closing with Compliance & Call-to-Action
*   **Disclaimers:** Scroll to the bottom disclaimer. Point out the environment, environmental clearances, TCP, and RERA approval wordings.
*   **Key Narrative:** Highlight our commitment to responsible planning. Inform them that the current preview allocation is restricted to 50 Founder Circle slots, and invite them to schedule a private, physical site walk in the valley.

## 3. Presentation Guidelines

### 🌟 What to Show First
1.  **Survey 109 boundary overlays:** Demonstrates thorough land intelligence, engineering precision, and respect for local topography.
2.  **1-Acre land allotment ratios:** Highlight that ~63% of the plot is dedicated to managed organic cashew agroforestry.
3.  **High-fidelity renders:** The double-height lounge, composite decks, and biophilic open-air courtyards.

### ⚠️ What to Avoid Showing
1.  **Internal spreadsheet details:** Avoid screenshots of margins, contingency formulas, or administrative dashboard views.
2.  **Unconfirmed walkthrough videos:** If showing 3BHK, 4BHK, or Customisable models, do not attempt to play the videos. Point out that the *walkthrough is undergoing final cinematic rendering* and highlight the static 4K galleries instead.
