Geospatial Context

Estate Terrain & Valley Context

Reserva Verde Goa is situated in a high-comfort biophilic forest valley in South Goa’s pristine Netravali region. Designed around the natural topography, all structures are raised on stilted screw-piles to preserve surface hydrology, native tree systems, and local contour runs.

✦

Natural Forest Valley Orientation

Bounded strictly by high ridge cashews and valley bamboo groves, creating absolute visual and acoustic shields from outside zones.

✦

Low-Impact Access Roads

Road networks follow natural animal and harvesting pathways, utilizing water-permeable local laterite stones to prevent soil damage.

✦

Terrain-Sensitive Layout Planning

Helical stilt placement strictly avoids mature tree root zones. Conceptual boundaries are subject to physical survey verification and statutory approvals.
