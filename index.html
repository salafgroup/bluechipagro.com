Planning and project status

Reserva Varde Goa is a conceptual eco-estate project by Bluechip Global Agro. The layouts, maps, villa designs, indicative prices and proposed estate allocations are preliminary. Title, land survey, zoning, conversion, environmental and forest clearance, applicable permissions, Goa RERA applicability and legal due diligence must be verified before a sale or development. No approval, return, income or appreciation is guaranteed.
