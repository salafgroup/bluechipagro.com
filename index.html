Artist's Impression

The One-Acre Estate Philosophy

Every private holding at Reserva Varde Goa consists of a full, demarcated acre of forest land. To preserve the ancient canopy and secure absolute privacy, the built-up villa footprint is strictly limited, leaving the remaining area for professional agricultural cultivation and native green buffers.
